Academic Calendar

Important dates and schedules for the academic year

Semester 1 (2026)

The first semester runs from January to April, covering the first half of the academic year with lectures, practicals, and assessments.

Orientation: January 6 - 10

Lectures Begin: January 13

Mid-Semester Break: March 9 - 13

End of Lectures: March 2

Exam Period: May 4 - 8

Semester 2 (2026)

The second semester runs from May to August, continuing with coursework, projects, and final examinations.

Lectures Begin: April 28

End of Lectures: July 29

Exam Period: July 11 - 27

Results Release: September 7

Semester 3 (2026)

The third semester runs from September to December, completing the academic year with final courses and graduation preparations.

Lectures Begin: September 14

Mid-Semester Break: November 2 - 6

End of Lectures: December 4

Exam Period: December 8 - 19

Graduation: December 20

Registration Deadlines

All students must complete registration within the specified deadlines. Late registration may incur additional fees and requires department approval.

Semester 1: January 6 - 17

Semester 2: May 4 - 15

Semester 3: September 14 - 25

Late Registration Fee: KSh 500

Public Holidays

The institute observes all Kenyan public holidays. Classes and administrative offices are closed on these dates.

New Year: January 1

Labour Day: May 1

Madaraka Day: June 1

Mashujaa Day: October 20

Independence Day: December 12

Christmas: December 25

Fee Payment Schedule

Tuition and other fees must be paid before the start of each semester. Payment plans are available for students who need financial flexibility.

Semester 1: Before January 13

Semester 2: Before May 4

Semester 3: Before September 14

Payment Plans: Available
